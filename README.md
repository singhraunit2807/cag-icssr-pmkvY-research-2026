## Primary Audit Source

Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG), **Report No. 20 of 2025, Skill Development under Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)**.

The official CAG source is used as the principal audit evidence for the study.

## Preliminary Research Focus

The study examines audit-identified issues relating to:

- Data integrity and data governance
- Beneficiary identity and eligibility verification
- Digital monitoring and system validation
- Data integration and interoperability
- Evidence retention and traceability
- Outcome and placement tracking

## Research Approach

The study is being developed through:

1. CAG audit evidence
2. Official government sources
3. Academic literature
4. Evidence-based analysis
5. Development of a digital-governance framework

## Evidence Discipline

The research will distinguish clearly between:

- Findings reported by the CAG
- Researcher interpretation of those findings
- Proposals developed from the analysis

No unsupported claims about fraud, corruption, cyberattacks, intentional misconduct, or causation will be introduced.
