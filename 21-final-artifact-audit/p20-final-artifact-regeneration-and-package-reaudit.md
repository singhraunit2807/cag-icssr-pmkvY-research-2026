## Important rendering caveat

The runtime PDF renderer may substitute a metrically compatible serif font if the actual Times New Roman font is unavailable in the environment. Therefore the safest final submission workflow is to open the DOCX in Microsoft Word on a system where Times New Roman is installed and export that DOCX to PDF, then use that exported PDF for submission.
