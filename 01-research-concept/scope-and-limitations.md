# Scope and Limitations

## Scope

The study focuses on digital-governance aspects of PMKVY implementation, particularly:

- Data quality and governance
- Beneficiary identity and eligibility verification
- Digital monitoring and system validation
- Data retention and traceability
- Centre-State and other data integration issues
- Monitoring, feedback, and outcome tracking

The study uses the national PMKVY programme as its main context. Jharkhand may be discussed where it appears in the selected-state audit evidence, but it is not treated as a standalone case unless the evidence supports such a design.

## Limitations

1. The principal evidence base is the CAG performance audit and related official sources.
2. Audit findings do not by themselves establish causal relationships between digital-governance weaknesses and employment outcomes.
3. State-level observations cannot automatically be generalized to all PMKVY implementation.
4. Proposed technical measures will be included only where they are supported by the evidence and research literature.
5. The final methodology and research gap will be finalized after systematic evidence and literature review.
