# Preliminary Research Concept

## Working Title

**Strengthening Digital Governance in Government Skill Development: An Audit-Based Study of Data Integrity, Beneficiary Verification, Monitoring and Outcome Tracking under PMKVY**

## Competition Context

This research is being developed for the **2026 CAG–ICSSR Research Article Competition**, National Level, under the theme **Skilling, Employment and Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)**.

## Research Context

The research uses the CAG's performance audit of the Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) as its principal evidence base. The study focuses on the governance of digital information and controls used for implementation, verification, monitoring, and outcome tracking.

## Preliminary Research Problem

The audit identifies weaknesses involving data completeness, validity, retention, beneficiary verification, system validation, monitoring evidence, and data integration. These findings raise a research question about how digital governance controls can strengthen the reliability of information used to administer and monitor a large-scale government skill-development programme.

## Central Research Question

What do the CAG audit findings on PMKVY reveal about gaps in data governance, beneficiary verification, digital monitoring and outcome tracking, and what evidence-based digital-governance measures could address these gaps?

## Preliminary Analytical Chain

**Digital system → Data quality → Beneficiary verification → Monitoring and evidence → Outcome tracking**

This chain is provisional and will be tested against the CAG evidence and academic literature before the final framework is fixed.

## Evidence Boundary

The study will not treat an audit observation as proof of fraud, corruption, cyberattack, intentional misconduct, or causal impact on employment outcomes unless the available evidence directly supports such a claim.

State-level findings will also not be generalized to the entire country without appropriate national-level evidence.

## Current Status

Preliminary concept completed. The next research stage is systematic extraction and verification of CAG evidence.
