# Preliminary Research Objectives

1. Examine data-governance and IT-control gaps identified in the CAG audit of PMKVY.

2. Analyse weaknesses in beneficiary identity and eligibility verification.

3. Examine how data-quality and monitoring weaknesses affect the reliability of programme oversight and feedback.

4. Assess the relevance of data integration, interoperability, and system-validation mechanisms for programme governance.

5. Develop an evidence-based digital-governance framework for strengthening monitoring and outcome tracking in large-scale government skill-development programmes.

## Status

These objectives are provisional and may be refined after the evidence matrix and literature review establish the final research gap.
