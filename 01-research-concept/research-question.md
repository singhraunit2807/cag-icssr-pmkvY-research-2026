# Preliminary Research Question

## Central Question

What do the CAG audit findings on PMKVY reveal about gaps in data governance, beneficiary verification, digital monitoring and outcome tracking, and what evidence-based digital-governance measures could address these gaps?

## Why This Question Matters

A research question defines the specific problem that the study will investigate. Here, the question connects the audit evidence to digital-governance analysis without assuming that every identified weakness caused an employment or placement outcome.
