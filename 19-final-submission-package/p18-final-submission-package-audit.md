## Important distinction
The official guidelines require the submitted Research Article itself to be a PDF. The DOCX is retained as the editable source. The runtime-generated PDF is a layout preview because the environment lacks the actual Times New Roman font file; the DOCX explicitly specifies Times New Roman throughout. Before upload, open the DOCX in Microsoft Word on a system with Times New Roman installed and export/save as PDF so the submitted PDF uses the exact required font.

## User-side final steps
1. Open the DOCX in Microsoft Word.
2. Confirm all text is Times New Roman 12 pt, double-spaced, with standard margins.
3. Export/save as PDF.
4. Open the exported PDF and inspect the title page, all four tables, Works Cited and final page.
5. Keep the article PDF blind: do not add name, university, student ID, email, acknowledgements or other identifying information.
6. Complete the bona fide/forwarding document through the university authority and obtain the required signature and official seal.
7. Upload the Research Article PDF and required supporting document(s) through the official ICSSR submission portal.
8. Before final submission, re-check that the correct National Level competition/category is selected.
9. Submit before 30 September 2026.
