# Process 4 — Research Methodology & Analytical Framework

## Status
100% COMPLETE

## Research design
Audit-based secondary research using:
1. qualitative document analysis,
2. descriptive quantitative analysis, and
3. comparative policy/document analysis.

## Primary empirical source
CAG Report No. 20 of 2025, Skill Development under Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY).

The main audit covered PMKVY 1.0–3.0 for July 2015–March 2022. The article must distinguish this main audit period from later PMKVY 4.0/SIDH developments and from the eight-state audit sample.

## Supporting sources
- MSDE/PMKVY official guidelines and monitoring documents
- PMKVY 4.0 guidelines
- Skill India Digital/SIDH official information
- DMEO/NITI Aayog
- OECD
- verified peer-reviewed literature

## Unit of analysis
The unit of analysis is a documented PMKVY governance/audit observation and its corresponding governance function/control, not an individual beneficiary.

## Analytical dimensions
### 1. Data quality
Completeness, validity, duplication, consistency and documented data anomalies.

### 2. Beneficiary verification
Identity, age, educational qualification, technical qualification and eligibility.

### 3. Data retention and evidence traceability
Retention policy, electronic records, photographs/videos, audit trail and evidence preservation.

### 4. Monitoring and system validation
Inspection evidence, geo-tagging, biometric systems, authentication, duplicate/reused evidence and system validation.

### 5. Interoperability/data integration
Centre-State data exchange, API/data consistency, common information systems and institutional coordination.

### 6. Feedback and outcome tracking
Beneficiary feedback, placement information, post-certification tracking and employment-related information.

## Analytical framework
Digital programme system
→ Data generation
→ Data quality
→ Identity/eligibility verification
→ Data retention & traceability
→ Monitoring & system validation
→ Centre-State data integration
→ Feedback
→ Outcome tracking
→ Programme oversight & course correction

The framework is derived from the programme lifecycle and CAG audit objectives/findings rather than introduced merely for technical presentation.

## Finding-analysis template
For each important CAG finding:
1. CAG finding
2. Exact numerical/documentary evidence
3. Period and population/scope
4. Governance dimension
5. Documented implication
6. Relevant governance principle/framework
7. Potential control requirement
8. Subsequent official reform, where relevant
9. Evidence on effectiveness, if available
10. Remaining evidence gap/limitation

## Quantitative analysis
Only descriptive analysis of verified CAG figures. Example:

Percentage = (Problematic records / Total records) × 100

No regression or causal statistical modelling is planned because the study does not possess a suitable individual-level research dataset and the research questions do not require causal estimation.

## Qualitative analysis
Thematic coding of CAG observations under:
- Data Quality
- Verification
- Retention
- Monitoring
- Validation
- Integration
- Feedback
- Outcome Tracking

## Comparative policy/document analysis
Historical audit evidence (PMKVY 1.0–3.0 / 2015–22) will be compared with relevant subsequent PMKVY 4.0/SIDH mechanisms.

Existence of a new mechanism will not automatically be treated as proof of effectiveness. Where effectiveness evidence is unavailable, the study will distinguish control design/intention from demonstrated effectiveness.

## Triangulation
Important claims will be checked through:
CAG evidence + official government documentation + academic/institutional literature, where relevant.

Core verification chain:
Claim → Source → Exact Evidence → Period → Scope → Interpretation.

## Tools
- Structured evidence matrix
- Spreadsheet for calculations and cross-checking
- Document-analysis/coding framework
- Reference management for MLA 9 citation control

No advanced technical tool will be claimed unless actually used for a necessary analytical task.

## Validity and reliability
Reliability will be supported through source hierarchy, evidence traceability, scope/period checks, triangulation and explicit distinction between source-derived fact, researcher interpretation and proposed governance measure.

## Limitations
- Reliance on secondary evidence.
- CAG's detailed findings have a defined audit scope and selected-state component.
- Main audit period is 2015–22.
- PMKVY 4.0/SIDH are subsequent developments.
- The study does not establish causal relationships between digital-governance weaknesses and employment/placement outcomes.
- Where effectiveness evidence for subsequent reforms is unavailable, only design/intended control can be assessed.

## Causality boundary
The study may conclude that CAG identified a control weakness, classify the weakness, discuss documented implications for information reliability/oversight, and compare it with governance principles or later controls.

The study will not infer fraud, corruption, intentional manipulation, cyberattack, fake beneficiaries, or causal effects on placement without direct evidence.
