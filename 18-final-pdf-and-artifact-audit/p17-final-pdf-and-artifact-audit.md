## Font caveat
The runtime environment does not contain an actual Times New Roman font file. Consequently, LibreOffice substitutes metrically compatible serif fonts when exporting the PDF. The DOCX itself explicitly specifies Times New Roman throughout; opening it in Microsoft Word on a system with Times New Roman installed and exporting to PDF will produce the required font.

Therefore, the rendered PDF is a layout/visual-preview artifact, while the DOCX is the exact-font submission source. We do not falsely classify the runtime-rendered PDF as an exact Times New Roman PDF.
